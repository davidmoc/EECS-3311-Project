
### **F01 — News Dashboard**

**Related Use Case:** UC01 — Browse Current News  
**Related Sequence Diagram:** SD03 — Browse and Explore Current News

**Classes involved:**  
`NewsView`, `NewsController`, `NewsService`, `NewsProvider`, `EventClusterer`, `NewsSummaryService`, `LLMClient`, `Event`

**Important methods:**  
`NewsController.getDashboard()`  
`NewsService.getDashboardEvents()`  
`NewsProvider.getRecentNews()`  
`EventClusterer.cluster()`  
`NewsSummaryService.summarize()`  
`LLMClient.generate()`

**Execution:**  
When the user opens the homepage, `NewsView` requests dashboard data through `NewsController`. `NewsService` retrieves recent articles through `NewsProvider` and passes them to `EventClusterer`, which groups articles referring to the same underlying event. `NewsSummaryService` uses `LLMClient` to generate a concise summary for each event. The resulting `Event` objects are returned to `NewsView` and displayed as easily scannable news cards.

### **F03 — Multi-Step Political Research Agent**

**Related Use Case:** UC03 — Conduct Political Research  
**Related Sequence Diagram:** SD01 — Conduct Political Research

**Classes involved:**  
`ResearchView`, `ResearchController`, `PoliticalResearchAgent`, `ResearchPlanner`, `ResearchSession`, `ResearchPlan`, `ResearchCommand`, `ToolManager`, `ConcreteSituationBuilder`, `DialecticalMaterialistResearchMethod`, `ResearchStoppingPolicy`, `ConcreteSituation`, `ResearchAssessment`, `ResearchResult`

**Important methods:**  
`ResearchController.research()`  
`PoliticalResearchAgent.research()`  
`ResearchPlanner.createInitialPlan()`  
`ResearchPlanner.revisePlan()`  
`ResearchCommand.execute()`  
`ConcreteSituationBuilder.updateSituation()`  
`DialecticalMaterialistResearchMethod.assess()`  
`ResearchStoppingPolicy.shouldStop()`  
`DialecticalMaterialistResearchMethod.synthesize()`

**Execution:**  
The user submits a research question through `ResearchView`. `PoliticalResearchAgent` creates a `ResearchSession`, and `ResearchPlanner` generates an initial set of research commands. The commands retrieve evidence through `ToolManager`, after which `ConcreteSituationBuilder` updates the system’s structured understanding of the issue. `DialecticalMaterialistResearchMethod` evaluates the developing situation for material relationships, historical development, contradictions, conflicting evidence, and unanswered questions. Its `ResearchAssessment` is returned to `ResearchPlanner`, which can revise the plan and perform further research. This loop continues until `ResearchStoppingPolicy` determines that the investigation is sufficiently developed or the research budget has been reached. The final situation is synthesized into a `MaterialistAnalysis` and returned as a source-grounded `ResearchResult`.

### **F11 — Marxist Theoretical Analysis**

**Related Use Case:** UC11 — Request Marxist-Theoretical Analysis  
**Related Sequence Diagram:** SD01 — Conduct Political Research

**Classes involved:**  
`PoliticalResearchAgent`, `DialecticalMaterialistResearchMethod`, `MaterialRelationAnalyzer`, `HistoricalDevelopmentAnalyzer`, `ContradictionAnalyzer`, `EvidenceEvaluator`, `TheorySearchTool`, `MaterialistAnalysis`

**Important methods:**  
`DialecticalMaterialistResearchMethod.assess()`  
`MaterialRelationAnalyzer.identifyRelations()`  
`HistoricalDevelopmentAnalyzer.traceOrigins()`  
`ContradictionAnalyzer.identifyContradictions()`  
`EvidenceEvaluator.testExplanation()`  
`DialecticalMaterialistResearchMethod.synthesize()`

**Execution:**  
The feature operates on the evolving `ConcreteSituation` rather than applying a theoretical interpretation only after research has ended. `MaterialRelationAnalyzer` examines class, production, institutional, and ideological relationships. `HistoricalDevelopmentAnalyzer` examines how the situation developed over time, while `ContradictionAnalyzer` identifies significant internal tensions and relationships. `EvidenceEvaluator` tests interpretations against the collected evidence and looks for contradictory material. The resulting analysis is synthesized into a `MaterialistAnalysis`, with relevant Marxist-Leninist theoretical sources retrieved through `TheorySearchTool`.

### **F15 — Personal Research and Reading Workspace**

**Related Use Case:** UC15 — Save and Resume Research or Reading  
**Related Sequence Diagram:** SD08 — Save and Resume Workspace Item

**Classes involved:**  
`WorkspaceView`, `WorkspaceController`, `WorkspaceService`, `Workspace`, `SavedItem`, `WorkspaceRepository`

**Important methods:**  
`WorkspaceController.save()`  
`WorkspaceController.getWorkspace()`  
`WorkspaceService.saveItem()`  
`WorkspaceService.getWorkspace()`  
`Workspace.add()`  
`Workspace.remove()`  
`Workspace.search()`  
`WorkspaceRepository.save()`

**Execution:**  
When the user saves a document, passage, event, annotation, or research result, `WorkspaceController` forwards the item to `WorkspaceService`.
