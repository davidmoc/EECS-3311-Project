## **1.1 Project Description**

### **1.1.1 Problem and Purpose**

The proposed project is an AI-powered political research and news analysis platform designed to help users develop a deeper understanding of current events, politics, history, and political theory through a Marxist analytical framework.

The project addresses two major problems with modern political news consumption. First, news outlets differ in their ideological assumptions and biases, which can influence how events are framed, which facts are emphasized, which sources are treated as authoritative, and which explanations are presented. For Marxist readers, mainstream reporting may describe the immediate facts of an event without examining it in terms of class relations, labour, capital, ownership, imperialism, or the material interests of the groups involved.

Second, modern news is increasingly presented through short-form articles, social media posts, headlines, and other formats designed for rapid consumption. These formats often provide limited historical, economic, and political context. As a result, understanding a current event may require independently researching numerous sources, learning its historical background, and connecting that information to relevant political theory.

The purpose of this project is to bring these tasks together into a single platform. Rather than simply presenting news or summarizing individual articles, the system will help users investigate political topics, compare how they are presented by different sources, obtain relevant historical context, and analyze them through a Marxist lens.

### **1.1.2 Target Users**

The primary target users are Marxists, socialists, and others interested in understanding political issues through a Marxist perspective. The platform is also intended for students and users who are learning Marxist political theory and want to understand how theoretical concepts relate to real historical and contemporary events.

The system will therefore be useful to both experienced users who want an efficient political research tool and less experienced users who want explanations of Marxist concepts alongside their application to real-world issues.

### **1.1.3 Agent Capabilities**

The AI agent will allow users to investigate current events, historical events, political issues, and Marxist theory through natural-language requests.

For current events, the agent will be capable of retrieving information from multiple sources, comparing different accounts of an event, identifying differences in framing and emphasis, gathering relevant historical context, and synthesizing the retrieved information into a structured explanation. It will then be able to analyze the issue using relevant Marxist concepts and connect the event to applicable political theory or historical examples.

The agent will also support broader research outside of current news. Users will be able to ask questions about historical events, political and economic systems, Marxist concepts, theorists, and texts. The agent will be able to retrieve relevant information, explain theoretical concepts, identify relationships between ideas, and demonstrate how Marxist theories can be applied to particular historical or contemporary situations.

Rather than treating every request identically, the agent will determine which information and tools are necessary to answer a particular question and perform the appropriate sequence of research and analysis steps.

### **1.1.4 Why an AI Agent Is Appropriate**

An AI agent is appropriate because producing a well-informed political analysis often requires a multi-step research process rather than a single question-and-answer interaction.

For example, when asked to analyze a current political event, the agent may first determine what information is required, retrieve reporting from multiple sources, compare their coverage, identify missing context, retrieve relevant historical information, find applicable Marxist theoretical material, and finally synthesize the collected information into an analysis.

The AI therefore acts as an agent that coordinates multiple components and tools to accomplish a larger research objective. Its responsibilities can include reasoning, planning, information retrieval, source comparison, tool selection, and multi-step task execution. This satisfies the project’s requirement that the AI perform meaningful agent behavior rather than merely sending a user’s prompt to an LLM and displaying its response.

An important principle of the system will be distinguishing between information obtained from sources and the Marxist analysis applied to that information. The system should allow users to identify the factual and source material on which an analysis is based rather than presenting the agent’s interpretation as though it were itself a reported fact.

### **1.1.5 AI/LLM Model**

The system will integrate at least one Large Language Model as its primary natural-language reasoning and generation component. The planned implementation will use an OpenAI GPT model through an API, although the specific model may be selected or changed during implementation.

The LLM will be responsible for tasks such as interpreting natural-language requests, reasoning about retrieved information, comparing source content, explaining political concepts, applying Marxist theoretical frameworks, and generating coherent responses.

### **1.1.6 Interaction Between the AI Model and the Software System**

The LLM will operate as one component of a larger agent architecture rather than functioning as the entire application.

User requests submitted through either the graphical user interface or command-line interface will be passed to an agent controller. The agent will determine how the request should be handled and coordinate the necessary components. Depending on the task, this may involve retrieving external information, searching a Marxist theory knowledge base, comparing sources, accessing stored research or conversation context, and supplying the resulting information to the LLM.

The LLM will reason over this collected context and generate an appropriate analysis or explanation. The application’s deterministic components will manage functions such as user input, data storage, retrieval, tool execution, source organization, and presentation of results.

This architecture will allow the application to combine traditional deterministic software with LLM-based reasoning, retrieval, memory, and tool use, consistent with the overall project’s expectation that the system combine these types of components.
